The generated projects are deliberately not committed. They remain under the local <code>output/generated/</code> tree with their compiler logs, structural reports, screenshots, visual feedback, immutable revision snapshots, and final selection records.

To reproduce a demo, launch the desktop interface, paste the linked Prompt, and run the same direct pipeline. Exact pixels or source hashes may differ when the provider is nondeterministic; compare the recorded structure, interaction, visual score, timing, Token use, and hashes instead of claiming bit-for-bit identity.
