Create a polished, fully playable 3D top-down rally racing game set in a snowy
alpine valley. This is a presentation-ready vertical slice, not a grey-box
prototype.

The player drives a recognisable red rally car using WASD or the arrow keys.
Implement acceleration, braking/reverse, steering, momentum, grip and mild
drifting. The car must collide with snowbanks, barriers and scenery rather than
passing through them. Use a smooth elevated follow camera that keeps the road
ahead visible. The objective is to pass through six ordered checkpoints and
finish one complete lap before a 90-second timer expires. Skipping a checkpoint
must not count. Show speed, checkpoint/lap progress, remaining time, controls,
and clear win/lose/restart state in a legible HUD.

Build a winding closed mountain road with broad curves, elevation changes,
lane-edge markings, start/finish markings, guard rails and snowbanks. Surround
it with layered terrain, visible topographic contour-line styling, dense varied
pine forests, rock outcrops, small cabins, signposts and distant mountain
silhouettes. Model the rally car from multiple shaped parts with four visible
wheels, windows, lights and a contrasting roof; do not represent it as a single
box. Give checkpoints and hazards strong readable silhouettes without making
them look like editor gizmos.

Use a cohesive stylised low-poly art direction: cool blue-white snow, dark
blue-green trees, warm cabin windows and saturated red/orange focal accents.
Include directional shadows, soft fog, atmospheric depth, varied materials,
subtle particles or snow trails, and composed foreground/midground/background
layers. The two capture cameras should look like attractive real gameplay
screenshots and immediately communicate racing, alpine scale and the objective.
Avoid flat empty planes, default materials, isolated primitive test objects and
debug-looking UI.
